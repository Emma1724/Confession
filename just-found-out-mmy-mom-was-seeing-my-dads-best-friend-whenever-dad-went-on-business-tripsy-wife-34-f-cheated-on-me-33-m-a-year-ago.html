# My Mom Was Seeing My Dad’s Best Friend Whenever Dad Went on Business Trips

I'm 25M, and my parents have been married for almost thirty years. My dad travels quite a lot for his business. Sometimes he's gone for two or three days, sometimes an entire week. It was always normal in our house. My mom would complain that she hated sleeping alone, my dad would call every night, and I'd occasionally stop by to make sure she was okay. I never thought there was anything strange about it. My dad has one best friend, Mark, who has been around since they were teenagers. Mark was at my parents' wedding, he helped my dad start his business, and he was practically considered family. I grew up calling him Uncle Mark even though we weren't actually related. I actually thought it was sweet that he checked on my mom while Dad was away.

Nothing seemed unusual. They talked about work, sports, old friends, and my dad's travel plans. At one point my dad jokingly told Mark, "Keep an eye on her while I'm gone." Everyone laughed. Mark looked at my mom for half a second before laughing too. I didn't notice anything at the time. The next evening, I stopped by my parents' house after work because my mom had asked me to help with something on her computer. When I arrived, Mark's car was already in the driveway. My mom told me he'd come over because she was having trouble with a leaking pipe in the kitchen. I thought nothing of it. Mark had always been the guy everyone called when something needed fixing. He stayed for dinner, and afterward I went home. Looking back, that was the first coincidence I should have questioned.

The next time my dad traveled, Mark showed up again. This time, my mother told me he had come by because she wasn't feeling well. I remember joking with her and saying, "Dad really did leave you with a babysitter." She laughed and told me Mark was just being helpful. I didn't think anything of it. A few days later, my dad came home and thanked Mark for checking on her. Mark smiled and said, "She's family. Of course I was going to make sure she was okay." My dad hugged him. I remember watching them and thinking how lucky my parents were to have friends who cared that much. I had absolutely no idea that I was looking at the beginning of something that would eventually tear our family apart.

Again, I didn't immediately connect it to Mark. My mom had always been private about her phone, so I didn't think much of it. I didn't know what it meant. I told myself it could have been about anything. Maybe they had been talking about an old friend. Maybe they'd stayed up discussing something important.
