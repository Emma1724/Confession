An Unthinkable Online Encounter With My Uncle

Published by Anonymous (18F) | True Confession

While my parents were away visiting relatives in the Philippines for the holidays, I stayed home alone in Canada. Chatting casually on Facebook with my 46-year-old uncle started innocently enough as he shared photos of family trips. Seeking advice about modesty for an upcoming visit, I asked if a bikini was appropriate and sent a photo from the back, which led to compliments on my weight loss and physical changes.

What began as casual conversation quickly escalated through boundaries I never expected to cross. After asking for more photos of bathing suits and praising my figure, his firm, demanding tone instructed me to pose in specific ways. Caught up in the sudden rush of excitement, I followed his commands, sending intimate pictures and eventually engaging in a live video call session where we both acted on the intense thrill of the moment.

While the physical sensations and climaxes were undeniably intense, dealing with the aftermath has left me deeply conflicted, harboring intense guilt, shame, and confusion. Knowing he is married with children, part of my extended family, and that I have a boyfriend I am keeping this from makes the secret feel overwhelming and impossible to navigate.
