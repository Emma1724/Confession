A Forbidden Family Secret That Has Lasted 5+ Years

Published by Anonymous | True Confession

It all started before my brother married his current wife. One night over beers, my brother casually suggested I pursue his soon-to-be mother-in-law, who was going through a divorce. Sparks flew instantly when we met the next weekend, and we hooked up that very night. What began as a spontaneous encounter turned into an ongoing routine leading up to the wedding, with daily hookups and snatched moments anywhere we could find privacy—even sneaking away during the wedding reception.

Though an accidental discovery by her younger son nearly blew our cover years ago, and family members asked us to stop, we never truly did. Over five years later, we still slip away whenever we can, picking up right where we left off between other relationships. Out in public at family functions we act completely normal, but behind closed doors, our connection remains as intense as ever.

We know we can't date openly and we know we have to keep our secret safe, but the chemistry is impossible to walk away from. She is funny, adventurous, and an amazing person, leaving me wondering how long we can keep this secret going.
