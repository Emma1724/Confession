Until one day when I was about to hop into the bath and decided to get scissors to open a face mask. So before hopping in the tub I walk out of the bathroom completely naked and there she was sitting in our living room. I yelp and jump into the adjacent room and hobbled together some clothes from some dirty laundry that had been left in there before scurrying out. By then she then was sitting on the opposite side of the room with her back facing where I’d been, clearly embarrassed.

I grab the scissors and she left after getting something from my husband. I then scolded my husband for not warning me. But he didn’t think I’d come out of the bathroom and she’d be quick so he didn’t bother warning me. So I guess me getting spotted naked once was a worthy price to pay for us both having uninterrupted privacy for the rest of our lives. Now I have a funny story on top of privacy and we’re still close, we just don’t bring that one incident up.
