My mom is sleeping with her best friend's husband and has shown no remorse after getting caught

Published by Anonymous | Family Confession

Hello, everyone. I (22F) currently live with my mother (53F). After graduating from high school at 18, I moved to another city to study for three years. During that time, I visited my mom quite often. Last year, I moved back in with her.

In December of last year, I started noticing that my mom was secretly messaging and calling her best friend's husband. She wasn't particularly good at hiding it, and every time I caught her doing it, I felt sick to my stomach.

By April of this year, the situation had started taking a serious toll on my mental health. I began experiencing panic attacks at random times throughout the day. To cope with the constant anxiety, I started smoking, even though it goes against my personal morals. When I realized how badly this situation was affecting me, I decided to seek therapy.

I went into therapy hoping to find some kind of relief or guidance. I had already played out every possible scenario in my head, but I couldn't find a solution that didn't leave me feeling trapped. Unfortunately, therapy didn't seem to help me with this particular situation.

I'm religious to an extent, and whenever I prayed, I found myself asking for one thing: for my mom to get caught. There's something important you need to understand about my mom's best friend. She is closely connected to my mom's entire friend group, and they are practically family. If the affair came to light, my mom would risk losing all of her friends.

I couldn't bring myself to expose her myself. She's the only parent I have, and I'm currently living in her house. I feel trapped because I can't confront her about what I know without potentially making my living situation even more difficult.

Two days ago, I received a message from her best friend's eldest daughter. She told me that I should ask my mom to distance herself from her parents because my mom was causing problems in their relationship. She even told me that I could ask my mom about it. Before I could respond, she blocked me. At that point, it was pretty obvious that the affair had been discovered, at least by some of the family.

Surprisingly, I felt relieved. After months of anxiety and silently watching my mom betray someone who trusted her, I felt like karma was finally catching up with her. I know that might sound cruel, but I had been carrying this burden for so long that knowing the truth was coming out felt like a weight being lifted off my shoulders. I didn't mention the message to my mom. Instead, I decided to wait and see how things would unfold.

When she came home from work, she seemed completely normal. At one point, she practically sprinted upstairs, and I had a feeling she was going to call him. I've always avoided listening in on their conversations because I was afraid of triggering another panic attack. However, that day, I decided to listen for the first time.

What I heard made me feel physically sick. They were discussing how they could cover their tracks and continue the affair despite having been caught. At one point, my mom even giggled during the conversation.

The thing is, my mom has absolutely no idea that I know about the affair or that I've overheard their conversations. As far as she knows, I'm completely unaware of what's going on. I've been keeping everything to myself, pretending that nothing is wrong while this situation eats away at me from the inside.

I genuinely cannot comprehend how someone can betray their best friend like this, get caught, and still have so little remorse. Hearing her laugh while discussing how to continue the affair completely changed the way I see her. I'm disgusted by her behaviour, and I'm struggling to reconcile the person I thought my mother was with the person I'm seeing now. Right now, I feel more hatred towards her than I've ever felt towards anyone else.

The worst part is that I can't simply walk away. I cannot afford to move out, and I'm still living under her roof. I'm stuck in a situation that is destroying my peace of mind, and I don't know how to cope with it anymore. I feel like I'm living a double life. Every day, I have to act as though everything is normal around my mom, knowing what she's doing behind everyone's backs. I can't talk to her about it, I can't bring myself to expose her, and I don't know how much longer I can keep pretending that I don't know anything.

I don't know what to do. How do I deal with this situation when I can't move out, can't bring myself to expose her, and have to live with someone whose actions have completely changed how I feel about her? Any advice would be greatly appreciated.
