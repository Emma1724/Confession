I had an affair with my aunt's husband?

Published by Anonymous | Family Drama Confession

I flew across the country and showed up at the airport unannounced and uninvited to visit my aunt to stay for 2 weeks around Christmas. I had not seen them for 17 years. There was one night when I was alone with my aunt's husband when she and everyone else left. He drunk me and we did some things together. This was the first time we had any interaction for 17 years.

My aunt came home a few hours later and didnt see anything happen. We slept in the bedroom and her husband slept in the living room. In the morning, her husband came into the room and was banging the doors, stomping his feet, making noises trying to wake me up. I was laying next to my aunt. I was laying in a way that she was next to me and I faced the other way, and she couldn't see my face. She covered her face so that he couldn't see her eyes. He was looking at me and waved at me. She caught him and said, "Why are you calling her like that?"

She asked me what happened that night and we even went to the police station. She put two and two together and knew something happened that night. She said, "Huh? What? You've been here for two weeks and you guys never talk except saying hello. You're not acquainted with him and suddenly he wanted to talk to you?"

Then she told me that she realized something. She asked me if I was looking at him and I said no. She caught me lying and said, "You were looking at him. You told me that you were sleeping. No one would wave to someone who is sleeping. Your eyes were open, you were looking at him and I guess you were smiling."

She asked me if it was consensual or if he forced me. I lied and said that it wasn't consensual and that he forced me. She said, "I will hold you on that. How did he force you? Did he hurt you?" And when I talked to my cousins and told them what happened, I was even smiling and laughing. One night when my aunt and I were sleeping beside each other, I guess I was moaning in my sleep because she woke up and said, "Hey! Why are you making those noises? Are those the noises you made with my husband? Did you said, "UGH! UGH! UGH!"?

Later on, she was throwing leftovers at me for me to eat. Like one time, my cousin's son was eating fries and she said, "Wait! Don't throw it away! She can eat that!" Another time my aunt and I were eating burgers and I finished mine first, and she threw her already eaten burger in front of me angrily. Why was she handing me leftovers to eat like dumpster trash? Because that was like a metaphor that I was literally getting her leftovers?

How about when she said, "No one would wave to someone who is sleeping." Is that true? How did she know something happened? How was she able to see through all my lies and knew that it was consensual? Because a wife's intuition is always right? Because when you're 60 years old, you are not naive and gullible? Why did she throw leftovers at me to eat like dumpster trash?
